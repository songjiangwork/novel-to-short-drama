Prologue
Mara arrived before sunrise, carrying a letter she had refused to open for three days.

The station was nearly empty, and the last night train had already gone.

Chapter One
At nine o'clock, Mara finally opened the letter in the cafe across from the station.

The message contained only an address, a date, and her brother's name.

Chapter Two
By noon, she was standing outside the old theatre named in the letter.

A man she did not recognize opened the side door and said he had been waiting for her.

Epilogue
Mara folded the letter once more and stepped inside.
